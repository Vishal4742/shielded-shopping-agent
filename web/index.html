How auto-pay works

Approve one rule. Every run after that passes the same checks in code, and you get a minute to skip it.

Flow in order: 1, the rule, approved once. 2, limits per order, per day and bot-wide, plus a 30 second gap. 3, the SSA_PAUSED kill switch. 4, a Bitrefill invoice paid from store credit. 5, delivery of talktime to the phone. 6, a shielded receipt only your viewing key reads. FAQ

Before you approve a rule.

Can the agent spend more than I approved?

No. A rule names one operator, one number and one amount. Each run goes through the same checks in code as a manual buy: your daily cap, the total daily cap, the 30 second gap, the operator's kill switch and your balance. The language model only reads manual messages and never starts a run. There is no override: raising a limit means changing the code.

How do I stop a rule?

Tap Stop on any heads up message, or send /stop with the rule number. /rules lists every rule you have running.

What if I miss the heads up?

The run goes ahead. The heads up is a chance to skip, not a gate. If the bot was down when a run was due, that run is skipped rather than bought late in a burst.

Who holds the funds?

In this testnet build the operator keeps the wallet seed. You still get your own shielded account with its own viewing key, so your receipts never mix with anyone else's. Moving custody to you is the next milestone.

What does Bitrefill learn?

The number and amount it has to deliver. It is paid from a prefunded balance, so it never sees your Zcash address, your Telegram account or your other purchases. We refill that balance from shielded ZEC through NEAR Intents, which takes one transparent transfer: one pooled refill from us, not one per user.

Does the bot hide where it runs?

With Tor switched on, wallet sync and receipts go through Tor, so the light wallet server sees a Tor exit instead of our IP. Telegram, Bitrefill and /report still connect directly.

Why testnet ZEC?

It is a hackathon build, and testnet ZEC has no market value. The recharges themselves are real, which is why the ₹200 daily cap covers everyone together.

How do I read my receipts?

Send /viewkey. Import that key into Zingo or zcash-devtool and each memo appears, for example SSA·7c41e0·jio·₹10. Or send /report: the bot builds a watch-only wallet from that key alone and sends back a CSV of your purchases. You can also hand the key to an auditor.
