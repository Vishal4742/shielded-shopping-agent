FAQ

Questions people ask at the table.

Who holds the money?

In this testnet build the operator holds the wallet seed. Each person gets their own shielded account inside it, with its own viewing key, so receipts never mix. A non custodial version is the next milestone.

What does Bitrefill see?

The phone number and the recharge amount, because it has to send the recharge. It never sees your wallet, your balance or your other purchases. It is paid from a prefunded balance, not from your address.

Can the AI spend my budget?

No. The model only turns your message into operator, number and amount. Code checks every limit, and nothing is bought until you tap Buy on the quote.

What if the bot goes offline mid order?

An order whose outcome is unknown is marked as being checked and is never retried on its own. The operator confirms it with Bitrefill, and a failed order puts the money back in your budget.

Why testnet?

It is a hackathon build. The ZEC you deposit is testnet ZEC with no market value. The recharges are real, which is why a hard daily cap of ₹200 applies to everyone.

How do I read my receipts?

Send /viewkey to the bot. Import that key into Zingo or zcash-devtool and you will see each memo, for example SSA·7c41e0·jio·₹10.
